Human attention broker

AgentNotify

Your coding agents can now ask for input, report blockers, and announce completed work without getting lost in another terminal or virtual desktop.

Installed and ready
1

Send a notification

Open PowerShell, Command Prompt, or a new WSL shell and run:

agentnotify.exe send ^
  --type input_required ^
  --priority high ^
  --title "Need your decision" ^
  --message "Choose option A or B."
In Bash/WSL, replace each ^ with \, or put the command on one line.
2

Teach your coding agent

Download or copy the agent skill, then place it in your agent’s skills directory. For Codex: ~/.codex/skills/agentnotify/SKILL.md.

You can always find the same actions by right-clicking the AgentNotify tray icon.

3

Use the right signal

Action-required types stay visible; completion and informational notifications dismiss automatically.

input_required
permission_required
blocked
error
completed
success
warning
info
agentnotify.exe health
agentnotify.exe list --unresolved
agentnotify.exe resolve NOTIFICATION_ID
